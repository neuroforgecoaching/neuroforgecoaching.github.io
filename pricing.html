Important Information About Coaching

NeuroForge provides educational and coaching services. Coaching is not physical therapy, medical care, psychotherapy, diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional. Services do not include individualized medical treatment or physical therapy. Coaching does not establish a healthcare provider-patient relationship.
