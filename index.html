A Different Perspective

What if your relationship with pain could change?

Persistent pain can gradually become the center of everyday life. You may find yourself thinking about symptoms, anticipating discomfort, avoiding activities, or constantly searching for the next thing that might make the pain go away.

When this happens, life can begin to feel smaller. Activities that once felt natural may start to feel uncertain or intimidating.

NeuroForge offers educational coaching to help you better understand persistent pain, explore how attention, expectations, stress, and behavior relate to your experience, and develop a more confident approach to everyday life.

The goal isn't to organize your entire life around eliminating symptoms. It's to help you move toward a life that matters to you.

Meet the Founder

A background in physical therapy. A focus on understanding pain.

NeuroForge was founded by Paul Giordano, PT, DPT, whose background includes physical therapy and an interest in pain science, nervous system education, and the ways people respond to persistent symptoms.

The NeuroForge coaching approach draws on concepts from pain science, behavioral education, attention, and self-management to help people develop a more informed and less fear-driven relationship with their experiences.

NeuroForge is a separate educational coaching service. Coaching services are not provided as physical therapy or as a substitute for healthcare.

The NeuroForge Method

Understand. Explore. Reconnect.

Our approach focuses on education, awareness, and meaningful behavior change. Rather than making symptom elimination the only measure of progress, we explore how you can respond to difficult experiences with greater understanding and confidence.

01

Understand Pain

Learn about modern pain science and how the brain and nervous system contribute to the experience of pain.

02

Explore Attention

Examine how attention, expectations, and symptom monitoring can influence the way you experience and respond to sensations.

03

Recognize Patterns

Explore the relationship between stress, daily habits, avoidance, and the way you respond to difficult experiences.

04

Build Awareness

Learn general educational approaches to mindfulness, curiosity, and observing sensations without automatically reacting to them.

05

Reconnect With Life

Identify meaningful activities and personal goals that help you build a life less organized around symptom monitoring.

06

Develop Independence

Build knowledge, confidence, and self-management habits that you can continue using beyond coaching.
